# Semi-live sync and scripted suggestions

The existing authenticated one-minute /api/cron schedule remains the synchronization driver. Vercel must actually execute it with CRON_SECRET. Shopify webhook setup already exists but subscriptions must be activated separately. Gmail Pub/Sub push and Supabase Realtime are not implemented by this change; the interface continues its existing polling transport.

Gmail hydrates up to 20 conversations within its 45-second budget. History changes take precedence over initial backlog entries. Failed entries keep retry state; unread/archive changes do not erase carryover obligations. Subject forms such as `16451 Rugtomize` are recognized alongside explicit `Order #16451` subjects. Inbound email text never authorizes account or production changes.

After Gmail hydration and scheduled provider work, deterministic suggestions are prepared for status/tracking requests with an exact email/order binding and Shopify facts checked within 15 minutes. All known package labels must be present before tracking suggestions. Label-created wording distinguishes creation from carrier acceptance. Production wording requires verified approval, artwork, a current confirmed date, and explicit verified per-rug dimensions/quantities. More than one rug with both dimensions at least 8 feet uses two days; otherwise one day. Unverified measurements are flagged rather than inferred from ambiguous filenames.

Suggestions live inside SHUTTLE Review, not Gmail drafts. They remain blocked from sending, require canonical-signature and whole-conversation review, and never infer customer approval, move files, edit invoices or purchase labels. Owner edits are preserved. Stale generated text is withdrawn when required facts stop matching. Cases without sufficient evidence receive an explicit needs-context reason instead of an invented answer. Latest SENT evidence suppresses routine suggestions; complete cross-thread duplicate checks still belong to final sending verification.

This is an initial rule set, not the complete approved template library or a claim of 80–85% coverage. Next work: import owner-approved exact templates/signature; collect verified productionFacts.units from established artwork/packing rules; add reviewed rules for other inquiry types; measure draft coverage and owner corrections before widening automation. Gmail backlog failures require production logs to diagnose. Do not turn on sending merely to clear the backlog.
